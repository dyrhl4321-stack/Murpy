Hello App Review,

Thank you for your patience. Our previous reply said nothing else was hidden, and that was wrong. After your last message we audited the whole app screen by screen and found three more issues. All of them are fixed in build 1.0 (11) and on murpy.app.

1. Placeholder payment screens. A crew entry fee, a crew "boost" and an unused subscription screen showed KRW prices. The crew screens then showed "payment complete" even though no payment took place. They were unfinished placeholders, and we removed them completely. There are no prices, purchases or paid features anywhere in the app.

2. Code-unlocked characters. Two limited characters could only be unlocked by typing a code we handed out at offline events. A reviewer had no way to get that code. We removed the code entry and the codes. Nothing in the app can be unlocked with a code.

3. App identification. Build 10 still added a "PWAShell" token to its user agent, set an "app-platform" cookie and overrode document.referrer, so the website could tell the App Store build apart from Safari. The website no longer used these markers, but they were still in the binary. Build 11 removes all three, so the website cannot detect the app at all.

The App Review notes now list every mechanic that is not immediately visible and how to reach it (three achievement characters, one easter egg, seasonal cosmetic items, operator-only moderation tools). The demo account is a regular account.

We would be grateful for a review of build 1.0 (11). If anything still looks inconsistent, please tell us which screen and we will fix it right away.

Best regards,
Hyunsu Kim, Murpy
