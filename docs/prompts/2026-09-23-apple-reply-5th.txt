[1] Resolution Center 답장 (영문, 그대로 붙여넣기)

Hello App Review,

Thank you for the detailed report. All three issues are fixed in build 1.0 (12) and on murpy.app.

1. Guideline 2.1(a) — blank screen after Sign in with Apple.

This was our bug and we are sorry it cost you a review cycle. The app is a web app in a WKWebView. Sign in with Apple opens the Apple consent screen with window.open(). Our WKUIDelegate answered every window.open() by loading the requested URL into the main web view and returning nil, so the app navigated itself onto the sign-in relay page and lost window.opener at the same moment. The relay page has no visible content and no opener to return the credential to, which is the blank screen you saw. The same bug blocked Google sign-in, which is why the demo account also failed.

Build 12 gives sign-in popups a real second web view created from the configuration WebKit provides, presented as a sheet. window.opener survives, the credential is returned, and the popup closes itself. We verified Sign in with Apple and the full signed-in app.

2. Guideline 2.1 — demo account.

The previous demo account used Google sign-in, and Google asks for an additional device verification when signing in from a new device or country. We cannot switch that off for your device, so we have added email and password sign-in, which has no such step. It is on the sign-in screen for everyone on every platform, labelled "이메일로 로그인" (Sign in with email).

The account below is already fully set up, so it goes straight into the app with no onboarding:

  User name: applereview@murpy.app
  Password: (see the App Review Information section)

Google, Kakao and Sign in with Apple all still work; the email option is simply an additional path.

3. Guideline 1.2 — anonymous user generated content.

Our 대나무숲 ("bamboo forest") board shows posts under a pseudonym. Every post is still tied to a signed-in account, and we have now put all four safeguards in place:

  - The app's age rating is now 18+.
  - The author can delete their own post at any time, immediately, from the post itself; the post and its comments disappear from the feed for everyone at once.
  - Every post, comment, image and user can be reported from a report sheet that states we act within 24 hours. A post reported by several users is hidden automatically while we review it. We remove offending content and eject the account that posted it.
  - The report sheet now shows our contact address, murpyapp@gmail.com, so anyone can reach us directly about inappropriate activity. It is also in Settings.

Users can block any other user, and blocked users disappear from the feed, matching and chat.

Please review build 1.0 (12). If anything still looks wrong, tell us which screen and we will fix it right away.

Best regards,
Hyunsu Kim, Murpy

[2] App Review Information — 메모 (영문, 기존 메모를 이걸로 교체)
────────────────────────────────────────────────
Murpy is a community app for people who work out: find a workout partner, check in at a gym, join a group session, and keep a small pixel avatar world.

SIGNING IN
The demo account uses email sign-in. On the sign-in screen tap "이메일로 로그인" (Sign in with email), then enter the user name and password in the App Review Information fields above. The account is already set up, so it goes straight into the app. Google, Kakao and Sign in with Apple also work.

NOTHING IS FOR SALE
There are no purchases, prices, subscriptions or paid features anywhere in the app, and no payment of any kind. "머피" is an in-app point earned only by using the app (signing in, checking in, posting). It cannot be bought.

FEATURES THAT ARE NOT IMMEDIATELY VISIBLE
Everything below is reachable by any user; none of it is tied to the review process, a build, a date, a device or a user agent. The app behaves identically in Safari and in the App Store build.
  - Three achievement characters unlock from gym check-in records.
  - One easter egg: tapping the small dinosaur ten times on the matching screen.
  - One hidden field object: standing on the plywood in the gym field and tapping it 10 times, then 30 times.
  - Seasonal cosmetic items appear during a calendar month.
  - Moderation tools (report queue, content removal) are visible only to the operator account. The demo account is a regular user account, not an operator.

USER GENERATED CONTENT (guideline 1.2)
The 대나무숲 board shows posts under a pseudonym; each post is tied to a signed-in account. Age rating is 18+. Authors can delete their own posts immediately. Any post, comment, image or user can be reported; the report sheet states we act within 24 hours and shows our contact address murpyapp@gmail.com. Posts reported by several users are hidden automatically. Users can block other users.

PERMISSIONS
Location is used for gym check-in and for showing nearby gyms. Camera and photos are used for the workout photo feed and the profile picture. All are optional and requested only when the feature is used.

Contact: murpyapp@gmail.com
