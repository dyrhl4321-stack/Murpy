SIGN-IN
Use "Sign in with Apple" on the first screen. Google and Kakao sign-in are also available. No other sign-in path exists.

WHAT THE APP DOES
Murpy is a community app for people who work out in Korea. Users post workout check-ins, join small workout groups (Squads/Crews) and meet workout partners. Each user also has a pixel character and room (Murpy World) that they decorate with items bought with "Murpy" points, which are earned only through activity. Murpy points cannot be bought. There are no in-app purchases and no payments.

LOCATION-DEPENDENT FEATURES
Gym check-in uses location and only works near a registered gym in Korea, so a reviewer outside Korea will not be able to check in. Everything else (feed, Squads, Crews, Murpy World, open-world park, chat) works anywhere.

GAME MECHANICS THAT ARE INTENTIONALLY A SURPRISE (disclosed here in full)
- Achievement characters shown as "???" in the character list. They unlock automatically from check-in history:
  - Weekday Cult Leader: at least 6 check-ins in 8 weeks, mostly on the same weekday.
  - Gym Sommelier: check-ins at 5 or more different gyms.
  - Comeback Zombie: a check-in after a break of 14 days or more.
- Seasonal room items: unlocked by activity counts shown in the collection book (e.g. 8 posts). Some are delivered as a gift box in the user's room.
- Easter egg: after using all of today's match suggestions, tapping the dinosaur picture 10 times adds a dinosaur item to the room.
- Field objects: a floor plate in the open-world gym reacts when a character walks over it.
None of these change what the app does. They are cosmetic rewards only.

OPERATOR TOOLS
The developer's own accounts have moderation tools (review reports, block abusive users) and test tools for cosmetic content. Regular accounts do not have them. They cannot be enabled by any setting, URL or gesture.

USER-GENERATED CONTENT (1.2)
Report and block are available from every post, profile and chat (⋮ menu). Blocked users: My Page tab (bottom right) → "차단한 사람" (Blocked users), where users can unblock them. Account deletion: bottom of the My Page tab → "계정 삭제" (Delete account).
