[Login]
Tap "Google 로그인" and sign in with the demo account above (2FA is disabled). The account is already onboarded, so you land on the home feed. "Sign in with Apple" and Kakao are also on the login screen. There is no other sign-in path.

[What the app is]
Murpy is a fitness community from Seoul: workout photo posts, workout-partner matching, small meetups (Squads/Crews), gym check-in, and a pixel character + room (Murpy World) decorated with "Murpy" points.

[Nothing is sold]
There are no purchases, prices or paid features anywhere in the app. Murpy points are earned only through activity and cannot be bought. No external payment links.

[Changes in build 11 and on murpy.app since the last review]
- Removed placeholder crew screens that showed a KRW entry fee / boost price and a "payment complete" message without any real payment.
- Removed two limited characters that could only be unlocked with a code handed out offline. There is no code entry anywhere now.
- The iOS app no longer identifies itself to the website (no custom user-agent token, cookie or referrer). The App Store build and murpy.app in Safari cannot tell each other apart and show the same content.
- No remote switches, no review/region/date detection, no hidden modes.

[How to test]
- Home feed: post any photo to verify a workout.
- 매칭 (Matching): today's suggested partners, send a request.
- 스쿼드 (Squad): create or join a meetup.
- 체크인 (Check-in): works only near a registered gym in Korea (location permission), so it cannot be completed from outside Korea.
- 머피월드 (Murpy World): character, room, park open-world, group mini-games.

[Cosmetic surprises - fully disclosed]
They unlock cosmetic items only:
- Three "???" characters unlock from check-in history: 6+ check-ins in 8 weeks mostly on one weekday / check-ins at 5+ different gyms / a check-in after a 14+ day break.
- Matching tab: when today's suggestions run out, tap the dinosaur 10 times.
- Murpy World gym field: tapping the plywood floor gives a glowing aura.
- Seasonal room items unlock from activity counts shown in the collection book.

[Operator tools]
The developer's own accounts have moderation tools (reports, blocking abusive users). Regular accounts, including the demo account, do not. They cannot be turned on by any setting, URL or gesture.

[Report / block / delete account]
Report and block: ⋮ menu on posts, profiles and chats. Blocked users: My Page (profile icon, top right) → "차단한 사람". Delete account: bottom of My Page → "계정 삭제".
