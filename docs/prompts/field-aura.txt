■ 이게 뭔지
헬스장 필드 바닥 합판을 열면 나오는 보상. 캐릭터 "뒤편"에 깔리는 아우라 이펙트다.
바람의나라 스킬 이펙트 느낌. 시즌마다 다른 아우라로 교체한다.
캐릭터 본체는 절대 안 건드린다 — 아우라만 따로 뽑아서 캐릭터 밑 레이어로 깐다.

■ 뽑는 방법
아래 블록을 통째로 복사해서 붙여넣는다. 한 번에 하나씩.
A/B/C 중 마음에 드는 걸 고르면 되고, 셋 다 뽑아서 비교해도 된다.

■ 뽑고 나서 (내가 함)
- 배경 형광초록(#00FF00) 빼고 → 프레임 공통 bbox 로 잘라 정렬 → APNG 로 묶는다
- 캠프파이어·아기공룡과 같은 방식이다. 앱 코드는 한 줄도 안 바뀐다

A. 상승 기류 — 몸에서 김이 오르는 열기  (헬스장 시즌 추천)
================================================================

Pixel art sprite animation strip, 6 frames in a single horizontal row.
Total canvas exactly 846 x 224 pixels, each frame exactly 141 x 224 pixels.
Background: solid pure green #00FF00 filling every frame completely. No transparency, no gradient in the background.

Subject: a glowing heat aura that rises upward, designed to be drawn BEHIND a character sprite. The character itself must NOT be drawn — only the aura effect. Leave the vertical center column mostly empty so the character will sit there.

Style: 16-bit era pixel art, hard pixel edges, NO anti-aliasing, NO blur, NO soft gradients. Limited palette, chunky dithering only. Every pixel must be a solid color block.

Aura design: warm updraft. Wavy vertical streaks of energy rising from the bottom edge toward the top, widest and brightest at the bottom, thinning and fading as they rise. Small glowing embers drift upward between the streaks.
Colors: deep amber #8A4A12, mid gold #F5C24B, bright highlight #FFE9A8, and a few white-hot pixels. Only these hues plus the green background.

Animation across the 6 frames: the streaks rise and loop seamlessly — frame 6 must flow back into frame 1 with no jump. The overall silhouette height and width stay the SAME in every frame; only the internal pattern moves.

Composition: the aura is centered horizontally in each frame, roughly 120 px wide and 200 px tall, with an 8 px green margin on all sides. Keep the exact same position and scale in all 6 frames.

B. 회전 링 — 발밑에서 도는 기운의 고리  (바람의나라 보호막 느낌)
================================================================

Pixel art sprite animation strip, 6 frames in a single horizontal row.
Total canvas exactly 846 x 224 pixels, each frame exactly 141 x 224 pixels.
Background: solid pure green #00FF00 filling every frame completely. No transparency.

Subject: a rotating magic ring aura that orbits around where a character stands, designed to be drawn BEHIND the character sprite. Do NOT draw any character — only the ring effect. The vertical center must stay mostly open.

Style: 16-bit era pixel art in the visual language of classic Korean MMORPG skill effects. Hard pixel edges, NO anti-aliasing, NO blur, NO soft gradients. Solid color blocks with chunky dithering for shading.

Aura design: a wide flattened ellipse ring near the bottom (as if lying on the ground in perspective), plus a second thinner ring floating higher up. Short energy shards point outward from both rings. Faint spark pixels orbit along the ring paths.
Colors: deep blue #123A8A, action blue #3D7EFF, pale cyan #A8D4FF, and a few pure white pixels. Only these hues plus the green background.

Animation across the 6 frames: the rings rotate steadily and the sparks travel along them, looping seamlessly — frame 6 flows into frame 1 with no jump. Ring size and position are IDENTICAL in every frame; only the rotation phase changes.

Composition: rings centered horizontally, lower ring about 110 px wide sitting near the bottom of the frame, upper ring about 70 px wide at roughly two-thirds height. Same position and scale in all 6 frames.

C. 솟구치는 빛줄기 — 파워업 순간
================================================================

Pixel art sprite animation strip, 6 frames in a single horizontal row.
Total canvas exactly 846 x 224 pixels, each frame exactly 141 x 224 pixels.
Background: solid pure green #00FF00 filling every frame completely. No transparency.

Subject: a burst of energy beams shooting upward from the ground, designed to be drawn BEHIND a character sprite. Do NOT draw any character — only the beams. Keep the vertical center column mostly clear.

Style: 16-bit era pixel art, hard pixel edges, NO anti-aliasing, NO blur, NO soft gradients. Solid color blocks, chunky dithering only.

Aura design: several narrow vertical light beams erupting from a bright pooled base at the bottom. The beams have stepped, blocky edges (staircase pixel look, never smooth diagonals). Debris pixels kick outward from the base.
Colors: deep amber #8A4A12, gold #F5C24B, pale gold #FFE9A8, white highlights. Only these hues plus the green background.

Animation across the 6 frames: the beams pulse — growing taller and brighter, then settling — and loop seamlessly from frame 6 back to frame 1. The base pool stays anchored at the same spot in every frame.

Composition: effect centered horizontally, base pool about 100 px wide at the bottom, beams reaching about 190 px tall. Identical position and scale in all 6 frames.

■ 뽑을 때 자주 나는 문제 (안 되면 이 줄을 프롬프트 끝에 덧붙인다)
================================================================

- 캐릭터를 같이 그려버릴 때:
  Do not draw any person, body, silhouette, or character of any kind. Only the effect.

- 배경이 투명하거나 검게 나올 때:
  The background must be solid pure green #00FF00. Never transparent, never black, never white.

- 흐릿하게(안티에일리어싱) 나올 때:
  Every pixel must be a hard solid block. No smoothing, no blur, no soft edges, no gradients.
  Diagonal lines must be drawn as visible stair-steps.

- 프레임마다 크기·위치가 흔들릴 때:
  All 6 frames must share the exact same silhouette bounding box, position, and scale.
  Only the internal animation phase may change between frames.

- 프레임 개수가 안 맞을 때:
  Exactly 6 frames, evenly spaced, in one horizontal row. No frame borders, no labels, no numbers.
